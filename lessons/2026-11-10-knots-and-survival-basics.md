## Five knots

| Knot | Use | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Square (reef) | Joining two ropes of similar diameter | Not trustworthy for critical loads |
| Sheet bend | Joining ropes of different diameters | Dress it carefully |
| Bowline | Fixed loop that does not slip or jam under load and unties easily | Back it up if the load can shift |
| Taut-line hitch | Adjustable loop that grips under load; ideal for tent and tarp guy-lines | Slides when unweighted |
| Prusik | Friction hitch that grips a thicker rope under load; ascending, backup, adjustable shelter lines | Needs the right cord diameter |

## Rule of 3s

Roughly: 3 minutes without air or with severe bleeding, 3 hours without shelter in harsh conditions, 3 days without water, 3 weeks without food. Prioritise in that order.

## Distress signal

Three of anything: fires, whistle blasts or light flashes. On the ground, arrange them in a triangle.

## Water

- Boiling is the most reliable field method: a 1-minute rolling boil, longer at altitude.
- Do not ration water when you can treat more. Dehydration impairs judgment.

## North without a compass (northern hemisphere)

Point the hour hand of an analog watch at the sun. Halfway between it and 12:00 gives a rough north-south line. Treat this as approximate.
